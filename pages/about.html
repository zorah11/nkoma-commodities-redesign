About Nkoma

Ugandan coffee. Thoughtful trade.

An agricultural commodities company working to support, promote and export coffee grown and processed in Uganda.

Close to origin. Clear with buyers.

Nkoma’s role sits between Uganda’s coffee-growing regions and the buyers seeking dependable origin relationships.

The company’s work centres on sourcing, quality attention and export coordination. Rather than treating every coffee as interchangeable, the new Nkoma presentation gives buyers a clearer starting point for discussing origin, preparation and requirements.
