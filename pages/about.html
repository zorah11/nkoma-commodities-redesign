Nkoma Commodities Ltd is a Kampala-based agricultural commodities company focused on supporting, promoting and exporting coffee grown and processed in Uganda.

Close to origin.
Clear with buyers.

Nkoma’s role sits between Uganda’s coffee-growing regions and buyers seeking a direct, useful starting point.

Our presentation keeps the essentials visible: where the conversation begins, what kind of coffee is required, how it is prepared, the intended volume and destination, and the timing of the enquiry.

Details that depend on a specific lot or season are discussed directly rather than presented as permanent claims.
